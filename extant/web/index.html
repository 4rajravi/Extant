How an answer is made

  1. Find

    Searches only the paragraphs in force on your date, for your entity type.

  2. Set aside

    Drops superseded, out of scope and non-binding paragraphs, and lists every one it dropped.

  3. Verify

    Checks every quote against its paragraph and every number against its quotes. A claim that fails is withheld.
