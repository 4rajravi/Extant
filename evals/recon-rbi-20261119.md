# RBI reconnaissance, Phase 1 checkpoint

Steps 1 and 2 of the cached build graph, run on 18 and 19 November 2026. Phase 1 stops here
for a design review before the paragraph parser. Every number below comes from the local
cache under `data/rbi/` and can be regenerated offline from it. Quotes are verbatim from RBI
pages and are cited by RBI's own page id, as in `BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13105`.

## The four findings that change the plan

1. **No PDF could be downloaded.** Every Master Direction PDF lives on `rbidocs.rbi.org.in`, and
   that host answered the crawler's first request with an image CAPTCHA (HTTP 200, "This
   question is for testing whether you are a human visitor ... What code is in the image?").
   Extant does not answer or work around CAPTCHAs, so the host is recorded in
   `blocked_hosts.json` and was not contacted again. Step 2 (PyMuPDF) is built and tested but
   ran on **0 documents**. The same text is published as HTML on `www.rbi.org.in`, which serves
   the crawler normally, and the HTML carries a body for 266 of 271 direction pages. HTML
   has no page numbers, and `Paragraph.source_page` is `NOT NULL`. **Decision 1 has since made
   the HTML the source of record**; see the HTML extraction section.
2. **The 244 consolidated directions carry no consolidation annex.** Their repeal paragraph points
   to one central circular, RBI/2025-26/100, whose annex is a flat list of withdrawn circulars
   with no column naming the direction or paragraph each one went into. The only directions
   with a "List of circulars repealed" annex are five issued after the consolidation, and each
   of those lists exactly one predecessor direction. **The clean annex extraction rate is 0 of
   15 sampled consolidated directions, and 0 of all 240 listed ones.** That is far below
   the 60 percent go or no go line, and it is a property of how RBI published the
   consolidation, not of any parser.
3. **The withdrawal list is a live page, not a frozen annex.** It is one HTML page with no
   pagination, holding 9,458 rows today: 9,441 dated before 28 November 2025 (4 short of the
   published 9,445) plus 17 added since. Row serial numbers shift as rows are added at the top.
   The frozen annex would be the notification's PDF, which is on the blocked host.
4. **244 reconciles exactly by page id**, but through withdrawals and re-issues, and the
   documents are living: 164 of 271 carry "(Updated as on ...)" and most have earlier
   consolidated versions published alongside.

## robots.txt

`https://website.rbi.org.in/robots.txt` returns `301` to `https://www.rbi.org.in/` (the home
page, not a robots file). `https://www.rbi.org.in/robots.txt` returns **HTTP 418 "Unauthorised
Access"** from the site's firewall to every client, including an ordinary browser, while
normal pages return 200 to the crawler's honest User-Agent. There are no readable rules, so
nothing disallows the paths used here; RFC 9309 treats a 4xx robots.txt as no restrictions.
You approved proceeding on that basis. The crawler sends
`Extant/0.1.0 (research crawler for public RBI regulatory text; contact: <EXTANT_RBI_CRAWL_CONTACT>)`.

### How 244 reconciles

The 11 Department of Regulation listings show 240 directions dated 28 November 2025. The
withdrawal list links eight more directions of that date which have since been withdrawn:

- four "Prudential Norms on Declaration of Dividend" directions (ids 13065, 13091, 13110,
  13142), replaced by the 10 March 2026 versions now listed (13324, 13321, 13328, 13319);
- four co-operative bank directions re-issued under new ids with the same 28 November
  date: 12985 → 13202, 13009 → 13205, 13035 → 13204, 13037 → 13203.

240 still listed plus the 4 dividend originals gives **244**; the 4 same-day re-issues are the
same directions counted once. The press release explains the number: "244 Master Directions",
footnoted "Including 7 new MDs on Digital Banking Channels Authorisation for 7 types of REs
issued today". It also says about 3,500 circulars were consolidated, not the "over 3,800" the
spec quotes, with the rest "identified as obsolete".

Five pages have no HTML body: the four withdrawn co-operative originals above, and **13159,
Commercial Banks Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy**, whose page carries only the title,
"(1952 kb)" and a PDF link. For that direction the PDF is the only source.

## Numbering conventions

Sample of 17 directions chosen across all 11 categories, the smallest and largest by PDF size,
and every structural oddity found: 13159, 13705, 13105, 13127, 13101, 13061, 13047, 13017,
13016, 13203, 12987, 12983, 12955, 12937, 12928, 12925, 13328. Counts across all 271 pages
are given alongside.

**Chapters.** Roman numerals with a title: "Chapter I – Preliminary" (en dash), also
"Chapter-I Preliminary" (hyphen, no dash; ids 13016, 12955). 257 of 266 bodies have chapters;
9 have none and go straight to lettered sections. Chapters are marked up inconsistently: in
13105 seven chapter headings are `<p class="head">` and three ("Chapter I", "III", "IX") are
plain `<p>`. A parser that trusts the class drops Chapter III's 25 paragraphs into Chapter II.
Body text also says "Chapter IIIC of the Reserve Bank of India Act", so a heading match must
be anchored and short.

**Sections.** Lettered headings inside chapters: "A. Short Title and Commencement",
"B. Applicability", "C. Definitions".

**Paragraphs. Five conventions, not one.**

| Convention | Directions | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Body paragraphs numbered `1.` to `N.`, continuous across the whole document, never restarting per chapter | 246 | 13105: 1 to 67 across ten chapters; "paragraph 36" is cited by number |
| The same with no dot after the number | 3 | 12949: "1 These Directions shall be called ..." |
| Numbered headings, body in sub-paragraphs or unnumbered | 17 | 13017: heading "2. Applicability", body "(1) These Directions shall be applicable to ..." |
| Numbered headings with decimal sub-numbers | inside the above | 13034: heading "4. Definitions", body "4.1 In these Directions, ..." |
| No body at all | 5 | above |

Lettered paragraphs appear in 71 documents: 62A, 62B, 62C (13105), 80A and 102A to 102D
(12987). They are not only amendment insertions: 62A is already in the original 28 November 2025
text of 13105. 77 documents have a gap or a backward jump in body numbering. In
13016 the jump is numbered notes under a table inside paragraph 10 ("1. For a bank the total
shall match ..."), which a naive parser would emit as a second paragraph 1. In 13127 numbering
returns to 1 after paragraph 212, inside an annex.

**Sub-paragraphs.** `(1)`, `(2)` (arabic, about 12,000 occurrences), `(i)`, `(ii)` (roman, about
6,900), `(a)`, `(b)` (lower, about 2,400) and rarely `(A)`. Provisos are separate unnumbered
blocks, "Provided that, ...", which belong to the paragraph before. 120 documents use HTML
`<ol>` lists, whose item numbers are generated by the browser and **are not in the text**.

**Annexes.** "Annex I" to "Annex X" (13105), "Annex 5" (13400), each starting with a heading
and holding forms, tables and schemes, sometimes with their own paragraph numbering.

**Footnotes.** 165 documents. The "Updated as on" versions mark amended text with footnotes such
as "Substituted w.e.f. October 1, 2026, consequent to Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks -
Governance) Amendment Directions, 2026 dated July 14, 2026" (13105). The footnote marker is
sometimes glued to the text it marks: "1These Directions shall come into force ...".

Numbered units across the 266 bodies: **about 20,500**, median 54 per direction, smallest 0
(the three no-dot documents before handling them), 4 (12974), 6 (13203), largest 525 (13156).

> (ii) NBFC-ICC registered with the RBI under the provisions of the RBI Act, 1934; ... (12949)

## Effective date and "Updated as on"

Commencement is stated in 245 of 266 bodies, in a dozen phrasings. The most common, with counts
from the 216 pages profiled first:

| Phrasing | Count |
|---|---|
| "These Directions shall come into force with immediate effect." | 54, plus 16 lower case |
| "These Directions shall come into effect immediately upon issuance." | 28 |
| "These Directions shall come into effect from the date of issue." | 17 |
| "These Directions shall come into effect on the day it is placed on the official website of the Reserve Bank of India." | 14 |
| "These Directions shall come into effect from <date>." | 9, for example "from January 01, 2026" (13034, 13101) |
| "... with immediate effect, unless otherwise specified." and variants | about 25 |

Only the dated form gives `effective_from` directly. "Immediate effect" resolves to the issue
date printed in the page header, which is a deterministic reading of stated text. "The day it
is placed on the official website" names no date anywhere in the document. "Unless otherwise
specified" means some paragraphs carry their own dates, as the footnotes show.

`issued_on` is the date line under the RBI numbers at the top of each page, for example:

> RBI/DoR/2025-26/204 DoR.SOG(SPE).REC.No.123/13-04-001/2025-26
> November 28, 2025 (13105)

A re-issue can carry a later date than its listing: 13202 is listed under 28 November 2025 and
dated "December 04, 2025" on the page. "Updated as on" appears in the title of 164 of 271
pages, for example "Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Miscellaneous) Directions, 2025
(Updated as on October 19, 2026)".

## What a consolidation annex looks like

There are three kinds, and none is the one the design assumed.

**The central withdrawal annex.** RBI/2025-26/100 (DOR.RRC.REC.302/33-01-010/2025-26, 28 November
2025) says: "9445 circulars listed in the Annex, which are either circulars whose instructions
have been consolidated or circulars which have become obsolete / redundant, are withdrawn ...
with immediate effect and are hereby repealed." The Annex link goes to the live withdrawal page.
Its columns are `S No. | Circular Number | Circular Name/Title | Date`. It names no new direction,
no paragraph, and does not say which rows were consolidated and which were obsolete.

**Post-consolidation repeal annexes.** Five directions issued after 28 November 2025 have one,
for example 13328 (Small Finance Banks, Declaration of Dividend, 10 March 2026):

> 15. The list of circulars repealed with respect to the provisions relating to SFBs coming
> under the purview of this Direction is in Annex III.

with a table `Sr. No. | Circular Number | Date of Issue | Subject` holding one row,
`DOR.ACC.REC.118/21-02-067/2025-26 | November 28, 2025 | Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance
Banks – Prudential Norms on Declaration ...`. That maps a direction to the direction it replaced,
at document level.

**Old-format appendices.** The 14 pre-consolidation Master Directions (2015 to 2025) linked from
the withdrawal list do have appendices listing the circulars they consolidated, for example
`Sr.No. | Circular No. | Date` with rows like `DBOD.BP.BC.92/C.469-76 | August 12, 1976` (KYC,
id 11566). One, Financial Statements 2021 (12158), adds a column "Reference
chapter/paragraph/ annex", which is a paragraph level mapping. But all 14 were themselves
withdrawn on 28 November 2025, so they map old circulars into withdrawn directions. Nothing
published maps those to their successors among the 244.

Inside the consolidated directions the text barely cites its sources: across the 248 listed
directions with an HTML body there are 345 circular references, 226 of them the central
withdrawal circular and 119 to 43 other circulars.

## What is going to be hard

1. **Getting PDFs at all.** The CAPTCHA blocks the one source with page numbers. Without PDFs,
   `source_page` and `evidence_page` have nothing to hold, and 13159 has no text at all.
2. **Gold concordance labels do not exist for the 244** in the form the design assumed. This is
   the go or no go question, and the answer from the published material is no.
3. **Living documents.** Each direction has a current consolidated text plus dated earlier
   versions, with amendment footnotes carrying per paragraph effective dates. As of answers
   need the version history; identity needs a rule for what a version is.
4. **Five numbering conventions,** inconsistent heading markup, browser numbered `<ol>` lists,
   numbered table notes, decimal and lettered insertions. Each is a way to drop or duplicate
   paragraphs silently.
5. **Identity.** RBI numbers are per financial year (`RBI/DoR/2025-26/204`), the masthead
   number is missing from 17 pages, and same-day re-issues carry new RBI page ids.
6. **The withdrawal list changes under us,** and its serial numbers shift.
7. **Reference normalisation** has to cope with non-references and typos, not only "No.".

## Decisions before the parser

1. **Source of record. Decided 19 November 2026: the HTML direction pages.** PDFs were chosen
   first, but 250 manual downloads were not practical, so the HTML pages on `www.rbi.org.in`
   are the source of record and step 2 extracts them (`extract_html.py`, below). Consequences:
   - **No page numbers.** Paragraphs and citations are located by a source anchor instead;
     see decision 9, now done.
   - **13159 has no HTML text.** Commercial Banks, Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy exists
     only as a PDF. It can be downloaded by hand and filed with `import-pdfs`, which stays in
     place, together with the PyMuPDF extractor, for this kind of gap.
   - **Earlier versions are HTML too,** so decision 3 needs no PDFs.
2. **Label strategy. Decided 19 November 2026: published lists only.** The withdrawal list gives
   `repealed` with dates, the standalone list gives `retained`, and the post-consolidation repeal
   annexes give document level supersession. No paragraph level `consolidated_into` gold exists
   for the 244, and none is inferred. Mined; results in the section on labels below.
3. **Versions. Decided 19 November 2026: RBI's own history, and the earlier texts are crawled.**
   A direction's history page lists its earlier consolidated texts, "Updated as on <date>", oldest
   first; they are `v1` to `vN` and the current page is `v(N+1)`, each superseding the one before.
   Checked before numbering, on all 249 listed directions: every history is strictly ascending,
   no two versions share a date, and every current text is newer than its last earlier version.
   The current text's date comes from its title, which RBI writes in a few forms ("(updated as on
   October 7, 2026)", a closing "]"), or for 13159, which has no text, from its listing title.
   The 333 earlier texts are crawled and extracted like the current pages. Code:
   `identifiers.py` (`number_versions`); output: `derived/versions.jsonl`.
   Result: **582 versions** for the 249 listed directions, 249 current and 333 earlier, all
   333 crawled (332 requests, 0 failures). 92 directions have one version; the rest have 2 to 10
   (69 with 2, 40 with 3, 30 with 4, 7 with 5, 4 with 6, 5 with 7, 1 with 8, 1 with 10). For
   example 13105 is `rbi:md:2025:0204:v1` (28 Nov 2025), `v2` (1 Oct 2026) and `v3`, the
   current text (19 Oct 2026). The listing title fallback for the current date, used only for
   13159, agrees with the page title on all 156 directions that carry both.
   **Gap: 37 earlier versions in 30 directions have no HTML text,** only a PDF link on the
   blocked host (for example `NOTIFICATION_13162_28_11_2025.PDF`); 30 of them are the original
   28 November 2025 text, and for 5 directions every earlier version lacks text. As of answers
   for those directions before their first update cannot be grounded in the HTML source.
4. **Paragraph anchors. Decided 19 November 2026.** A paragraph is named by its printed number,
   `p62A`, `p4.1` (a space before an inserted letter, as in "38 A.", is dropped), and includes its
   sub-paragraphs, provisos, lists, tables and the numbered notes under them. An annex printed in
   the page is `annex<n>` and its paragraphs `annex<n>.p<number>`, a schedule or appendix keeps its
   word (`schedule1`), and text before paragraph 1 is `preamble`. Code:
   `extant/corpora/rbi/anchors.py`. Checked on the corpus: every printed paragraph number fits the
   grammar, and no direction restarts numbering per chapter (13135 looked as if it did; its second
   run of 1 to 8 is Annex I). Annexes printed in the page are rare, 20 directions with one each,
   because most annexes are separate PDFs. What does repeat a number inside a document, in 44
   directions, are footnotes set as plain paragraphs ("1 Substituted w.e.f. ..."), which the page's
   own `#F1` links identify, and numbered notes under tables; neither is a paragraph. Some
   directions also number headings rather than body text (13135's 8, 9, 10, 66, 68 and 74).
5. **doc_id serial. Decided 19 November 2026: the RBI number.** `RBI/DoR/2025-26/204` is
   `rbi:md:2025:0204`, the year being the first year of RBI's April to March financial year. 248
   of the 249 listed directions print the number in their masthead (always the page's first
   block). The one that does not, 13159, has no text at all; it takes its serial from the listing's
   PDF file name (`151MD.PDF`) and its year from the listing date, giving `rbi:md:2025:0151`. That
   fallback was checked before use: on all 247 directions with both, file name serial and masthead
   serial agree, and on all 248 with a masthead the listing date's financial year is the
   masthead's. The three printed series (`RBI/DOR/`, `RBI/DoR/`, plain `RBI/`) produce no
   collision. The withdrawal notification RBI/2025-26/100 is `rbi:circ:2025:0100`. The version
   suffix waits on decision 3. Mapping: `derived/identities.jsonl`; code:
   `extant/corpora/rbi/identifiers.py`. My earlier "missing on 17 pages" counted withdrawn and
   old-format pages; among listed directions only 13159 lacks it.
6. **Entity codes. Decided 19 November 2026.** The 11 listing categories keep the codes in the
   table above, `cic` being Credit Information Companies; NBFC subtypes, which only the NBFC
   directions' text names, are `nbfc_*`, Core Investment Companies being `nbfc_cic`. 17 subtypes,
   each labelled with the long form the directions print and each found in the text (in 3 to 27
   of the 35 NBFC directions): the layers `nbfc_bl`, `nbfc_ml`, `nbfc_ul`, `nbfc_tl`; `nbfc_d`,
   `nbfc_icc`, `nbfc_mfi`, `nbfc_factor`, `nbfc_ifc`, `nbfc_idf`, `nbfc_p2p`, `nbfc_aa`,
   `nbfc_cic`; and `nbfc_hfc`, `nbfc_mgc`, `nbfc_spd`, `nbfc_nofhc`. Excluded on the text's own
   evidence: NBFC-ND and NBFC-ND-SI ("From October 01, 2022, all references to NBFC-ND, NBFC-ND-SI
   and NBFC-D ...", retired categories) and NBFC-LSP (only in "NBFC-LSP Arrangements", lending
   service providers). A bare "CICs" is never read as Core Investment Companies, because the NBFC
   directions also use it for Credit Information Companies ("Membership of CICs"). 28 entity types
   in all, in `derived/entity_types.jsonl`; each direction's listing category, RBI's own
   classification and not the parser's reading of its text, in `derived/listed_under.jsonl`. A
   renamed listing header fails the build. Subtypes of other categories, such as UCB tiers, are not
   coded yet. Code: `extant/corpora/rbi/entities.py`.
7. **Withdrawal key. Decided 19 November 2026: canonical reference, circular date and title.**
   A reference plus date is not unique: 9 such keys cover 23 rows, because RBI lists some old
   notifications once per amendment under the original number and date ("Amended upto
   01.10.2002 - ..." beside "Amended upto 30.06.2005 - ..."). With the title added every row is
   distinct except two pairs that are one circular listed twice in different spellings
   (`DBOD.No.Dir.BC.8/13.03.00/2005-06` and `DBOD.Dir.BC.8/...`, serials 2813 and 2815; serials 3202
   and 3203), which are merged. The 154 rows with no reference are distinct on date and title.
   `withdrawal_key` is the sha256 of the three (`extant/core/identity.py`); migration
   `0003_withdrawal_key.sql` makes it the primary key, keeps `circular_ref` as published, adds
   `canonical_ref` as the join key and makes `circular_date` required. The `Withdrawal` model
   enforces the key. **Gold set: 9,439 withdrawals** (`withdrawals.jsonl`): the 9,441 rows the
   notification covers, less the 2 merges. The 17 rows added to the live list later stay out of it:
   the page states no withdrawing instrument or date for them, which the model requires, so they
   remain in `withdrawn.jsonl` with that reason. Four of them, the November 2025 dividend
   directions, could be dated from the March 2026 directions whose annexes repeal them; not done.
8. **Department of Supervision directions. Decided 19 November 2026: in the corpus.** The 64
   directions listed under `did=419` to `429`, all dated 31 July 2026, are crawled, extracted,
   identified, versioned and classified like the 249 Department of Regulation ones: 64 pages, 64
   version histories and 22 earlier versions, 150 requests, 0 failures. Every one prints its
   number in the masthead, all in the `RBI/DoS/` series, and none shares a financial year and
   serial with a Regulation direction or with another Supervision one (RBI numbers one sequence

   RBI/DoS/2026-27/221 (DoS.CO.PPG.66/11.01.005/2026-27) of July 31, 2026: "the 628 circulars listed

9. **What replaces the page number. Decided 19 November 2026 and done.** Migration
   `0002_source_anchor.sql` makes `paragraphs.source_page` nullable, adds `source_anchor`, and
   adds the constraint `paragraphs_located`: every paragraph has a page, an anchor, or both. It
   also adds a nullable `concordance.evidence_anchor` for evidence read from HTML. An anchor is
   `#<element id>` (the page's own id, such as `#F9`, preferred) or `block:<index>` (position in
   the extracted page), resolved in the page whose bytes hash to `documents.source_sha256`; the
   grammar is in `extant/core/identity.py`. `Paragraph` and `Citation` enforce the same rule,
   and `Citation.verify` refuses an anchor that differs from its paragraph's. As a numbered
   migration it shapes new builds only.
